# FAQ

## Why was my card charged twice?

It was not: the second line is the VAT, shown apart on the invoice. The total you paid is the one at the bottom.

## I forgot my password

Use the reset link on the login page. We never see your password: it is hashed before it is stored, so support cannot tell you what it was.

## Why did my request fail and then succeed?

Downstream services sometimes fail for a moment. We retry a few times with a growing delay before giving up, which is why a request can take a little longer and still succeed.

## Can I get the report for last month?

Yes: the monthly report counts the events of every customer and can be downloaded as CSV from the dashboard.
